Position Sizing

Never put all your eggs in one basket.

A simple rule: keep 70% of your PI as a long-term hold. Only trade the remaining 30%, and even then in smaller chunks.

This protects you if the market drops suddenly — you'll still have your core stack.

Volume Matters

Price moves on low volume are unreliable.

If PI goes up 20% but volume is very low, it could easily reverse. A strong move needs strong volume behind it to be sustainable.

Always check the 24h volume before making a trade decision.

Pi Is Still Early

Pi Network is actively developing its Open Mainnet.

Smart contracts went live in early 2026 (v23 upgrade). This is exciting for long-term value, but also means the ecosystem is still maturing.

Early stage = higher potential rewards AND higher risk. Be patient and don't over-invest.

Limit vs Market Orders

Market order: Buy/sell immediately at whatever price is available. Fast, but you might get a bad price.

Limit order: You set the price you want. The trade only happens if PI reaches that price. Slower, but you stay in control.

For most Pioneers, limit orders are the safer choice.
